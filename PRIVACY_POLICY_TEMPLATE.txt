Privacy Policy Template — Product Studio

Product Studio uses the camera to capture product photos and scan barcodes. Catalog
processing (background removal, polish, styles, markup, and export) runs on the
user's device.

Data stored on the device:
- Product photos captured or imported by the user
- Barcode/UPC values scanned or manually entered by the user
- Local settings such as image quality, branding, and capture options
- Optional brand-mark logo chosen by the user

Optional network use (only when the user chooses these features):
- Import from URL: the app downloads the image the user pastes or enters
- Online backgrounds: the app searches and downloads stock images from Pexels
  and/or Pixabay using API keys you configure. Downloaded backgrounds and
  photographer/license attribution are stored locally for reuse

Data sharing:
- The app does not automatically sell or share user data.
- Users control sharing/export through the iOS share sheet or selected file export option.
- Online background search sends search queries to Pexels/Pixabay under their terms;
  product photos are not uploaded for catalog processing.

Permissions:
- Camera: required for product photos and barcode scanning
- Photo Library: used only when the user chooses to save, select, or export images
- Network: used only for optional URL import and online background search
